    Before you start

    Nothing here is a formality. An application missing the resale certificate or proof of trade sits in the queue rather than moving through it, so it is worth gathering these five items first.

    • 01EIN / federal tax ID The number as issued to the legal entity, not to an owner personally.
    • 02State resale or exemption certificate A PDF or photo, current and readable, with the issuing state named. This is the legally load-bearing document in the file.
    • 03Proof of trade A storefront photograph, or a live store URL we can open. Marketplace-only sellers should have their seller handles ready.
    • 04Owner or officer photo ID Used to confirm the person signing controls the entity. We record that it was checked, never the number on it.
    • 05Ship-to states Everywhere you intend to ship. Some markets are restricted and we will tell you which before you finish, not after.

    Municipal markets

    We screen the cart by ZIP, not by state — a state-level rule misses a Manhattan or a Chicago ZIP. Tell us if you intend to ship into any of these.

    Restricted destinations in your selection

    New Jersey is blocked statewide. New York City, Chicago (Municipal Code 4-144-190) and Philadelphia are blocked municipally. Orders to those ZIPs will not clear the cart, and New York State carries the strictest marking regime in the country, actively enforced against sellers shipping in. You can still hold the account; those destinations simply will not ship.

    Read the marking and ZIP-block detail. Scoping summary, not legal advice — confirm with your own counsel.

    That is everything we need

    Most applications are reviewed within 5 business days. Applications arriving with a legible resale certificate and a working store URL are usually faster; the ones that stall are missing one of those two.

    1. 01Document check — days 1 to 2 We read the resale certificate against the issuing state's register and open the store URL. If something is unreadable we email you rather than declining.
    2. 02Trade and channel review — days 2 to 4 Channel declaration is checked against what we can see publicly. Ship-to states set your marking tier and the ZIP blocks on your account.
    3. 03Terms and account opening — day 5 Approved accounts open at Tier 3 — Stocking Dealer, 1+ units, with terms quoted separately. Volume tiers are earned on 12-month volume, not negotiated at signup.
    4. 04First order Sign-in unlocks net pricing, on-hand counts, case packs and the quick-order paste box. Your rep is named on the portal from day one.
